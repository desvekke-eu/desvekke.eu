Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Google Backup is a private, non-commercial project intended to create local backups of the project owner's Google account data. It is not offered as a service to other users.

2. Google Account Data

The application is intended to access the following Google account information with the owner's consent:

  • Gmail messages and associated metadata, for the purpose of creating email backups.
  • Google Drive files and document metadata, for the purpose of downloading and exporting files into portable formats.

3. Use of Data

Retrieved data is used exclusively to create personal backups and to verify their integrity. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or commercial purposes.

4. Storage and Security

Backup data is stored on privately managed local storage and protected through access controls. Google OAuth credentials are stored locally and are not published on this website.

5. Data Sharing

Google account data is not sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. This website does not provide access to the backed-up Gmail messages or Drive files.

6. Data Retention

Backup data is retained for personal archival and recovery purposes until the account owner chooses to delete it.

7. Access Revocation

The account owner can revoke the application's access to Google account data at any time through Google Account security settings.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

The intended use of information received through Google APIs is limited to personal backup and recovery. The application is designed to follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

9. Contact

For questions about this project or its privacy practices, contact the site owner through the contact information associated with this website.
